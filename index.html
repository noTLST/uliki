Uliki Clip Uploader

An internal desktop tool that prepares highlight clips from live streams and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Uliki Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services. It uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to YouTube channels that belong to the person running the tool. It is not a public service: there are no third-party users and no sign-up.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in once per channel through the standard Google sign-in window and grants permission to upload videos to that channel.
  2. The tool cuts short clips and long videos from recorded live streams on the owner's computer.
  3. Finished videos are uploaded to the connected channel with a title, description, thumbnail and subtitles.
